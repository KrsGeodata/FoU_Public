# Min Eiendom – Functional Scope (MoSCoW)

This document describes the functional scope of **Min Eiendom** using the **MoSCoW prioritization method**.  
The goal is to clearly communicate what the solution **must**, **should**, **could**, and **will not** provide from a user perspective.

---

## Must Have

These features are essential for the solution to fulfill its purpose.

- Present property-related information in a **clear and understandable way**
- Provide a **modern and user-friendly design**
- Ensure **easy navigation** and overall usability for all user groups
- Follow the municipality’s **visual identity and style guidelines**
- Make the interface **clear, structured, and easy to understand**
- Apply established **UX principles** to ensure accessibility for users with different needs
- Present the **most important information first**, with tabs or sections for additional details
- Give users access to **all relevant information needed for an application**, either directly or via clear links
- Provide access to **previous and ongoing cases** related to the property
- Display **contact information** for relevant caseworkers and neighbours

---

## Should Have

These features are important and add significant value, but the system can function without them initially.

- Display **zoning plans and building maps** related to the property
- Collect and present **rules and regulations** that define what is allowed to build on the property
- Provide **guidance and explanations** related to building regulations
- Offer a **clear overview of applicable regulations** in one place

---

## Could Have

These features are desirable and may be implemented if time and resources allow.

- Provide a **universally designed interface** that supports a wide range of users
- Offer **automated guidance** to help users understand building rules and restrictions
- Clearly inform users about **what they are not allowed to build**, based on zoning plans
- Provide a **simple check or form** that helps users understand whether an application is required
- Support **personalized content** based on the user’s specific property
- Integrate guidance from external public services into the user flow

---

## Won’t Have

These features are explicitly out of scope for this project.

- A separate page that only states that users can do whatever they want on their property according to the law, without providing contextual or property-specific guidance

---

## Notes

- This scope focuses on **user needs and capabilities**, not technical implementation.
- Integration with external tools or systems is treated as a **conceptual capability**, not a guaranteed feature.
- The prioritization may evolve as the project progresses.
