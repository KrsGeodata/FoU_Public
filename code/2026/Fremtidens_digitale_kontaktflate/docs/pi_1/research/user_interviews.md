# User Interviews – Min Eiendom

We conducted user interviews to understand users’ needs, expectations, and perspectives related to **“Min Eiendom”**.

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Interview Questions & Notes</strong></summary>

### 1. Previous Experience with Min Eiendom
- Most interviewees had **limited experience** using the site.
- Only 1 out of 3 remembered visiting the site (checked cadastral/property number).
- None clearly remembered the reason for visiting.

### 2. Expectations of the Site
- Expected general property information: cadastral number, property number, size, year built.
- Expected information on **property boundaries with neighbors**.
- Wanted **early clarification** on how the information can be used.

### 3. First Impressions
- Immediate impression: **clear and easy to use**.
- Further down the page: negative impressions due to outdated design (e.g., Kartverket’s site).
  
### 4. Desired Information
- Information about **rights granted on their property**.
- Tool for **caseworkers**.
- More specific info on zoning plans.

### 5. Missing Information / Functions
- Submitted applications did not appear under "cases".
- Wanted property-specific information (e.g., zoning plans, regulations).
- Neighbor lists not updated (some new owners missing for 6–7 years).

### 6. Neighbor List Functionality
- Map was **clumsy and unclear**.
- Only direct neighbors appeared automatically, sometimes including unrelated areas (e.g., parking lot).
- Wanted **alternative to PDF** – view directly in browser, especially on mobile.
- Filters often did not work or were unclear.

### 7. Key Information for Users
- Better explanations of **terms and guidance** for functions.
- Easier navigation and **clearer instructions**.
- Information about **renovation documents** (own and neighbors’).

### 8. Use of AI / Chatbot
- Mark the map to indicate planned changes and find relevant rules.
- Determine whether an application is needed.
- Highlight **relevant sections of regulations** automatically.
- Guide users to correct documents and relevant paragraphs.

### 9. General Feedback
- Current solution **requires prior knowledge** – should be usable by all.
- Navigation is difficult, even for municipal staff.
- Future solutions should leverage **maps to answer questions**.
- Request for a **smoother, less cumbersome workflow**.
- Waste management map was clear – should apply similar clarity to property/zoning maps.
- Filters and zoning explanations need improvement.

### 10. Prototype Feedback
- Tab-based navigation is preferred (similar to Kristoffer’s approach).
- eByggesøk integration:
  - Price concerns (1200 NOK)
  - Excessive permission requests perceived as scary/confusing
  - Site often **did not work**
  - Users want an integrated solution with **same functionality and better workflow**.

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Summary of Insights</strong></summary>

**Key Takeaways:**
- Users have limited experience and need guidance to understand **site purpose**.
- Navigation and terminology are **not intuitive**.
- Maps and filters are critical, but current implementation is **unclear or clumsy**.
- Users want **more property-specific info**, including:
  - Rights and permissions
  - Zoning plans
  - Neighbor lists and renovation documents
- AI/chatbot could improve usability by:
  - Guiding users through regulations
  - Highlighting relevant documents
  - Showing whether applications are needed
- Overall workflow needs **simplification and integration**, e.g., eByggesøk functionality built in.

</details>

---

<details>
<summary><strong>Recommendations / Next Steps</strong></summary>

- **Improve site clarity**:
  - Explain terms clearly
  - Provide guidance for first-time users
- **Enhance navigation**:
  - Use tab-based interface
  - Improve map interactions
- **Integrate workflows**:
  - Include application submission history
  - Combine eByggesøk functionality
- **Provide real-time property information**:
  - Updated neighbor lists
  - Zoning plans and relevant regulations
- **Consider AI-assisted guidance**:
  - Help users understand rules and required applications
  - Highlight relevant documents and map locations
- **Usability testing**:
  - Continue testing with diverse users to ensure accessibility for all literacy and language levels

</details>
